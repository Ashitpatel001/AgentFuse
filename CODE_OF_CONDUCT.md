# CortexHeal Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
The CortexHeal project is dedicated to providing an inclusive, respectful, and productive experience for everyone who contributes to or uses our software.

## Expected Standards
- **Respectful Communication**: Treat all participants with courtesy and professional respect.
- **Constructive Collaboration**: Offer and accept constructive feedback openly.
- **Focus on the Community**: Prioritize the long-term reliability and safety of autonomous systems.

## Reporting & Inquiries
If you experience or witness behavior that violates these principles, please reach out directly to the maintainers at `team@cortexheal.dev`.
